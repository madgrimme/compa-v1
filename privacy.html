Compa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Compa is designed to be a private, personal learning tool for every child. Our philosophy is simple: you own your data, always.

1. No Data Collection

The Compa application does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information to its creators or any third party.

2. Local and Cloud Storage

Your learning profile (compa_profile.json) is stored in two places: directly on your device for offline use, and securely in your own personal Google Drive for backup. We, the creators of Compa, do not have access to this file. It is yours alone.

3. Google Authentication

When you sign in with Google, you are authenticating directly with Google's secure servers. We only receive permission to create and manage the single compa_profile.json file in your Drive. We do not have permission to see any of your other files, documents, or personal information.

Compa is a gift. It is not a product, and you are not the product. Your privacy is fundamental to our mission.
