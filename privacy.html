Compa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains our principles regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information. Our philosophy is simple: you own your data, always.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Compa application ("the Service") does not create any user accounts on our own servers. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, age, or location. We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Google Authentication

Compa uses Google Sign-In to authenticate you and to store your learning profile in your own personal Google Drive. This process is handled securely by Google. When you grant permission, our application receives a temporary access token with a very limited scope:

  • It can only access files created by the Compa application itself.
  • It cannot see, read, or access any of your other files, documents, emails, or personal information in your Google account.

3. Data Storage

Your personal learning profile (a file named compa_profile.json) is stored in two places:

  • On your local device: For fast, offline access, your profile is saved in your browser's local storage.
  • In your Google Drive: For backup and to sync your progress across different devices, a copy of your profile is saved in your own Google Drive account.

We, the creators of Compa, do not have access to this file or the data within it. You have full control over this file and can delete it at any time from your Google Drive.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer at the email address provided during the app consent process.
