Compa

Our Mission

Compa is a free, personal learning companion built on one core belief: every child deserves an education that meets them where they are. It is a tool designed to empower, not to grade, inspired by a family of educators and a passion for real learning over rigid schooling.

Our Story

This project began as a collaboration with a dedicated tutor in South Africa who saw firsthand how standardized systems can fail students. In many places, children can advance in grade without achieving true literacy, leaving them further and further behind. Good teachers bridge this gap by creating unique curriculums for each child—an exhausting, unsustainable act of dedication.

Compa was built to do that heavy lifting. It uses AI to gently ascertain a child's true level of understanding through innocuous questions. It then creates personalized lessons without judgment, leaning into a child's own interests to make learning engaging and effective.

How It Works

Compa is an offline-first web application. Your learning profile is owned by you and stored securely in your own Google Drive, not on our servers. This ensures privacy and allows the tool to work anywhere, with or without an internet connection.
